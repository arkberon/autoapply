Three steps, then it runs itself

  1. 1

    Answer once

    About fifty common questions: contact details, work authorization, start date, pay. Saved on your computer and reused on every form.

  2. 2

    It finds and ranks

    It searches company job boards for entry-level roles in your cities, checks which postings are still open and ranks them by fit.

  3. 3

    It applies and reports

    Every day at your chosen time it fills and submits applications, then sends a summary: what it sent, and what needs you.

Everything a job hunt repeats

Finds new jobs by itself

Searches the Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever boards of companies you follow. No spreadsheet to update.

Always your latest resume

Link a Google Doc. A fresh PDF is pulled before every run, so editing the doc is all you do.

Private AI

A local model reads each form and writes short answers from your resume. Nothing about you goes to an AI company.

Ranked by fit

Every job sorted from Apply first to Long shot, and by work area: software, IT, support and more.

Watches career pages

Follow pages that list many jobs; it shows only the postings that are new since you last looked.

Receipts for everything

Each application saves the time, the confirmation, every answer sent and screenshots before and after.

Autopilot that knows when to stop

When it can't answer something safely, it parks the job and tells you exactly why. Answer once, and it never asks again.

  • Legal questions are answered only from what you saved
  • Submit is pressed once; a crash can never apply twice
  • CAPTCHAs and emailed codes are left for you
  • Written answers use only facts from your resume
  • A daily limit and a pause between applications
  • Sites that forbid bots are never automated

Set it up in ten minutes.

Node.js, a local model from Ollama, and your resume as a Google Doc. That's all it needs.
